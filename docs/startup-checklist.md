# Lobster Middleman → Whatnot: Startup Checklist (prepared Oct 15, 2026)
S = SOURCED (URL below) · E = ESTIMATE / verify by phone

## 1. Can you sell live lobster on Whatnot? **Yes, with conditions.**
- Whatnot's Animal policy allows live **lobster** (it's one of the few live animals listed) (S). The seller must follow shipping rules and hold any required permits in both the seller's and the buyer's jurisdiction.
- Perishable food may be sold **only in the Meat & Seafood (or Specialty Food) category**, which needs **Whatnot approval. Whatnot replies within 5–7 business days** (S, policy updated Aug 20, 2026). Fresh/live seafood (lobster named explicitly) needs **1-day shipping**; no shipping temperature-controlled food Fri/Sat/Sun (S). It's U.S.-only. Fresh/live seafood must ship **1-day**; frozen can ship 1- or 2-day. Whatnot perishable labels are **UPS Next Day Air / Saver / 2nd Day** (S). If spoilage comes from picking the wrong shipping method, you can be billed for it.
- **Shipping:**
  - **UPS accepts live crustaceans including lobster**, but only on a guaranteed next-day service, and **never shipped on a Friday or before a holiday** (S). Box must be new, ≥275-lb burst / 44 ECT, marked "LIVE ANIMALS" (or "LIVE LOBSTER") on two opposite sides in ≥1-inch letters (S).
  - **FedEx does not list lobster** as an accepted live animal (fish, reptiles, amphibians and insects only), and it requires packaging pre-approval (S).
  - **USPS does not list lobster** as a mailable live animal (S).
  - **So: ship UPS Next Day Air, Monday–Thursday only.** Pack in a styrofoam box with gel packs and damp newspaper or seaweed. No standing water.
- **Interstate:** California's lobster rules don't bar out-of-state sales of legal commercial lobster. The buyer's state may have its own rules (E). Ship whole (no tails) and ≥3¼ in carapace (S, 14 CCR 121.5: lobster must stay measurable until sold to the ultimate consumer; only licensed processors/fish businesses may split it lengthwise, with both halves kept together).
- **Simplest alternative while approval is pending:** take orders on Whatnot only after you're approved. Meanwhile, sell locally to restaurants and markets, and offer local pickup in person.

## 3. Money and timeline
**Cash you can spend tomorrow (Oct 16):**
- Required: LLC $70 + EIN $0 + seller's permit $0 = **$70**.
- Optional: +$350 for the 24-hr expedite (S).
- **Total ≈ $70–$420**, plus an insurance down payment (~$400, E) and the CDFW Receiver License, $1,063 (online or at a sales office; covers 2026 only).
- **Realistic day-one total: ≈ $1,533–$1,883.**

**Total to be fully legal, first year:**
- Sourced: LLC-1 $70 + LLC-12 $20 + FTB $800 (due Jan 15, 2027) + FTB $800 (2027 year, due Apr 15, 2027) + Long Beach $473.47 + CDFW 2026 $1,063 + CDPH $874 = **$4,100.47**.
- UNVERIFIED: CDFW 2027 Receiver License, assumed $1,063 (needed Jan 1–Mar 17, 2027).
- Estimated: insurance ~$4,000 + HACCP course ~$400 = ~$4,400.
- **Total ≈ $9,563**, before equipment (tanks/coolers $3–8k, E). With $5,000 of equipment that's **≈ $14,563**, matching the calculator.

**What blocks selling:**
1. **CDFW license.** It's illegal to buy from fishermen without it.
2. **CDPH registration / HACCP plan.** Required to distribute seafood.
3. **Whatnot category approval.** 5–7 business days.

**Can you sell tomorrow? No.**
- Earliest realistic legal sell date: **~late Oct to mid-Nov 2026** (E).
  - Whatnot approval ≈ Oct 23–27.
  - CDFW license, if done in person ≈ late Oct.
  - CDPH registration is the long pole. Ask CDPH whether you may operate once the application has been filed and before inspection.
- **Fastest real path:** buy from a licensed wholesaler under the cheaper Wholesaler License instead of at the dock. That removes E-Tix. **CDPH registration is still required.**
- The season runs until Mar 17, 2027 (S, 14 CCR 121), so from a ~Nov 16 start you'd have about 17 weeks (~4 months) to sell.
